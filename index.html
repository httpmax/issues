Status. Fixed on live = fixed on the live site. Fixed on staging = fixed and waiting to go live (dashed outline). Partly fixed = some of it is still left. Waiting on decision = needs a business decision first.

Severity. Critical = security, other companies' data, wrong money or legal exposure. High = core business logic is wrong or users get stuck. Medium = missing logic or alerts. Low = display, wording or polish.
